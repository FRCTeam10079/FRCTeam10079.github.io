Our History

A young team, moving fast.

2025, Rookie Season (Reefscape)

ArrowDynamics took the field for the first time in the 2025 Reefscape season. Our rookie year ended on a high note: at the PNW District Championship in Cheney, Washington, the team earned the Rookie All-Star Award, which celebrates a young team that shows outstanding potential and embodies the spirit of FIRST. This award brought us to worlds, where we had a superior auto.

2026 Rebuilt Season

In our second season, the team kept climbing. At the PNW District Glacier Peak Event we reached the event finals and earned the Rising All-Star Award, then earned it again at the PNW District Auburn Event, and a third time at the FIRST Washington District Championship.

This fall we are carrying that momentum into off-season events, Bordie, Girls Gen, and Block Party, while we train even more members.
