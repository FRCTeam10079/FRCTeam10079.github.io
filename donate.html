Your Support is Important

Your generous contributions let us keep teaching students the skills and values that are the foundation of the FIRST community, and keep our robot on the field.

Student-Powered, Community-Funded

ArrowDynamics is run by students with the support of volunteer mentors. The team depends on fundraising, grants, sponsorships, and donations to cover competition registration, materials, and tools for every season.

Robots are Complicated

Every year we design, machine, wire, and program a brand-new competition robot in a matter of weeks. Motors, controllers, metal, electronics, and event fees add up fast, your support is what makes it possible.

Donate Directly to Bothell High School

Give straight to our Bothell High School Robotics account through the Northshore School District's TouchBase system. If you don't have a student in NSD, register a guest account first.

Use this path after signing in:

Items At All Schools → High School → Bothell High School → Robotics → Donations for Robotics

The school district adds a $2.95 processing fee to your donation before checkout.
