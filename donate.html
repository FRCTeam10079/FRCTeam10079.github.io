Donate Directly To Bothell High School

Donate directly to our Bothell High School Robotics account through the Northshore School District. If you are not a family with a student in NSD, you will need to sign up for a guest account first.

Register Guest Account Sign In To TouchBase

Use this path after signing in:

Items At All Schools -> High school -> Bothell High School -> Robotics -> Donations for Robotics*

Please note: The school district charges a nominal processing fee of $2.95, which is added to your donation before checkout.
