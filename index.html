We are a student-led robotics team from Bothell High School. Our mission is to excite and motivate students to pursue careers in science, engineering, and technology, and to build the leadership, business, and life skills that go with them.

Rebuilt Season Info

In our second season, ArrowDynamics reached the event finals at the PNW District Glacier Peak Event and earned the Rising All-Star Award three times, at Glacier Peak, at Auburn, and at the FIRST Washington District Championship.

This fall we will continue to grow the team by competing in off-season events such as Bordie. If you would like to watch us compete, feel free too. If you want to join us, we are always looking for new members.
