Who Can Join

ArrowDynamics is open to all Bothell High School students. Whether you are into mechanics, programming, electronics, design, photography, or business, there is a place for you on the team, students run every part of it.

Membership is established by paying the ASB fee and the Robotics Club fee, and maintained by regular attendance at club meetings.
